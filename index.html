The Costa de la Luz is literally the Algarve continued — the same Atlantic coast, the same wide soft-sand beaches with dunes and pine forest behind them, just on the Spanish side. Bolonia, Zahara de los Atunes, and Tarifa are consistently rated among Europe's best beaches, and they're noticeably emptier than Algarve in peak August. We anchor the trip with three world-class cultural stops: Sevilla (cathedral + Alcázar + flamenco), the Costa de la Luz beach base, and Ronda + the Pueblos Blancos (white villages built into cliffs) with the Sierra de Grazalema for hiking.

Why this arc beats the Catalan loop: sandier beaches (Atlantic dunes vs Mediterranean coves), warmer water, deeper cultural anchors, fewer crowds in Aug, and the food is on another level — pescaíto frito, tuna ronqueo, sherry from Jerez, gazpacho from the source.

Stop 1 · Sevilla & Córdoba day trip

Jul 21 – 24 · 3 nights · Andalucía's capital · 35 min from SVQ airport · base for Córdoba day trip

UNESCO Cathedral + Alcázar Tapas + flamenco Plaza de España Córdoba day trip

Why here

Sevilla is the cultural soul of Andalucía: Gothic cathedral with the world's largest altarpiece, Mudéjar palace gardens at the Real Alcázar (a Game of Thrones filming location), tapas alleys in Santa Cruz, and flamenco peñas with no tourist filter. July is hot — plan mornings outdoors, siesta indoors, evenings on rooftops.

Day by day

Tue Jul 21 · Arrival
  • Morning flight CDG → SVQ (2h45 direct on Air France, daily)
  • Taxi or bus EA from airport to centre (35 min, ~€25 taxi)
  • Check in, lunch at Bar Alfalfa or Las Golondrinas II
  • Easy afternoon wander through Barrio Santa Cruz (the old Jewish quarter)
  • Sunset rooftop drinks at Hotel Doña María or EME Catedral facing the Giralda
  • Dinner + late flamenco at Casa de la Memoria (book ahead — small, intimate)
Wed Jul 22 · Cathedral + Alcázar + Plaza
  • Early entry to Cathedral + Giralda climb (open 9am, beat the heat)
  • Late morning at Real Alcázar — palace + gardens (book skip-the-line online, 2h)
  • Long siesta lunch + nap
  • Late afternoon at Plaza de España — boat ride in the canal, tile murals of every Spanish province
  • Tapas crawl in Triana across the river — try Las Golondrinas, Casa Cuesta
Thu Jul 23 · Córdoba day trip
  • Morning AVE Sevilla → Córdoba · 45 min · ~€20 pp
  • Mezquita-Catedral — the breathtaking Moorish prayer hall with a cathedral grown inside it
  • Lunch in the Judería at Casa Pepe — try salmorejo (thicker, more luscious gazpacho — Córdoba's specialty)
  • Walk the Roman bridge over the Guadalquivir, see the Alcázar de los Reyes Cristianos
  • Late afternoon AVE back to Sevilla
  • Final dinner at Mariatrifulca with bridge views
Fri Jul 24 — transit day: Sevilla Santa Justa → Cádiz AVE · 1h45 direct · then 45 min bus or car to Vejer de la Frontera. Total ~3h door-to-door. Tip: rent a car at Cádiz or Sevilla station — you'll want it for beach-hopping the Costa de la Luz.

Stop 2 · Costa de la Luz — Vejer base

Jul 24 – 29 · 5 nights · Atlantic coast, the wide-sandy beaches you came for · base in or near Vejer de la Frontera, day-trips to Bolonia, Zahara, Tarifa, Cádiz

Wide Atlantic dunes Bolonia · Zahara · Tarifa Roman ruins on the beach Tuna ronqueo · sherry

Why here

This is the sandy-beach payoff. The Atlantic coast of Cádiz province has the longest, widest, sandiest beaches in Spain — backed by pine forest and dunes, washed by Atlantic swell, refreshed by the levante and poniente winds. Bolonia has a 4-km wall of fine sand and a giant rolling dune you can climb. Zahara de los Atunes is a fishing village with a beach you can walk for an hour. Tarifa is the kitesurf capital of Europe — and you can see Morocco across the Strait of Gibraltar.

Day by day

Fri Jul 24 · Arrival + Zahara sunset
  • Train Sevilla → Cádiz (1h45 AVE), pick up rental car or transfer to Vejer (45 min)
  • Settle into the Airbnb, late lunch in Vejer's main plaza
  • Drive 25 min to Zahara de los Atunes for first beach + sunset swim
  • Dinner at El Campero in Barbate (or Almadraba in Zahara) — bluefin tuna country, you're 5 min from where they catch it
Sat Jul 25 · Bolonia + Baelo Claudia
  • 20-min drive to Bolonia — park, set up on the wide sand
  • Beach morning + climb the giant Bolonia dune (it dwarfs the kids in photos)
  • Lunch at Las Rejas at the south end of the beach — toes-in-sand, fresh fish, gazpacho jugs
  • Afternoon at Baelo Claudia Roman ruins, right behind the beach — an entire Roman fish-salting town preserved, free entry for EU residents
  • Late swim, then back to Vejer
Sun Jul 26 · Tarifa + Africa across the Strait
  • 30-min drive to Tarifa — Europe's southernmost point on mainland
  • Walk through Tarifa Old Town (white-washed, mellow surfer vibe)
  • Lunch at El Lola or Surla
  • Option A: Beach afternoon at Punta Paloma (wide sandy bay with hot springs the locals know about) or Valdevaqueros (kitesurfer-watching)
  • Option B: Fast ferry Tarifa → Tangier, Morocco (35 min) — one of the wildest day trips you can do anywhere. Souk, mint tea, lunch in Africa, ferry back by 7pm. Bring passports.
Mon Jul 27 · Cádiz day trip
  • 1h drive (or train Vejer-area → Cádiz) to Cádiz — 3,000-year-old port city, Phoenician + Roman + Spanish layers
  • Walk the seafront promenade, La Caleta beach, Castillo de San Sebastián
  • Cathedral with the gold dome on the seafront
  • Lunch at Casa Manteca in La Viña — the best chicharrones de Cádiz with manzanilla sherry
  • Back to Vejer for evening Old Town tapas crawl at El Jardín del Califa + La Brasa de Sancho
Tue Jul 28 · Slow Vejer day + Trafalgar
  • Sleep in. Breakfast in Vejer's plaza
  • Late morning walk to Cabo de Trafalgar lighthouse (where the 1805 battle happened)
  • Beach at El Palmar (long sandy, surfer-friendly) or back to Bolonia for round 2
  • Sunset at the lighthouse — bring wine
Wed Jul 29 — transit day: Vejer → Ronda · bus is the best path (~2h direct on ALSA / Comes), or train via Algeciras (~3h with change). If you've got the rental car, the 2h drive through olive country is the scenic move. Drop the car in Ronda.

Stop 3 · Ronda & Sierra de Grazalema

Jul 29 – Aug 1 · 3 nights · Cliffside city + Pueblos Blancos + Mediterranean mountain hikes

Puente Nuevo + El Tajo gorge Pueblos Blancos Sierra de Grazalema Bull-ring + bodegas

Why here

Ronda is the most dramatic city in Spain — a white-washed town split by the 100-metre El Tajo gorge with the iconic Puente Nuevo bridge spanning it. Hemingway and Orson Welles both loved it; the bullring is Spain's oldest. Use Ronda as a base for the Pueblos Blancos (Setenil de las Bodegas, Grazalema, Zahara de la Sierra, Arcos de la Frontera) and the Sierra de Grazalema Natural Park — a limestone range full of cork-oak forest, vulture vistas, and Mediterranean hiking that's easier on kids than the Pyrenees.

Day by day

Wed Jul 29 · Arrival + Puente Nuevo at sunset
  • Arrive Ronda, settle in
  • Late afternoon stroll across the Puente Nuevo, descend to the viewpoint below for the iconic photograph
  • Visit the Plaza de Toros (Spain's oldest bullring, now mostly a museum)
  • Dinner at Bardal (Michelin) or the more casual Tragatá by chef Benito Gómez
Thu Jul 30 · Pueblos Blancos circuit
  • Rent or use the car: half-day loop through three white villages
  • Setenil de las Bodegas — houses literally built into and under the cliff overhangs, eat lunch on a street that runs through the rock
  • Zahara de la Sierra — perched above an emerald reservoir, swim in the lake below if hot
  • Grazalema village — the rooftops, the embutidos shops, the cork forest viewpoints
  • Back to Ronda for evening tapas
Fri Jul 31 · Sierra de Grazalema hike day
  • 30 min drive to the Sierra de Grazalema Natural Park
  • Easy family hike: Río Majaceite trail (Benamahoma → El Bosque, 4.5 km along a river, mostly flat, shaded)
  • Moderate: Salto del Cabrero loop — limestone cliffs, vulture vistas (12 km, half-day, kids 10+ ok)
  • Ambitious: El Torreón summit — Cádiz province's highest peak at 1,654m (permit required, book ahead at the visitor centre)
  • Lunch in El Bosque or Benamahoma at a venta — try the local trout, fresh from the river
  • Final dinner back in Ronda at Pedro Romero opposite the bullring
Sat Aug 1 — exit: Train Ronda → Sevilla Santa Justa (~2h direct) · transfer to Sevilla SVQ airport (20 min) · fly SVQ → CDG (2h45 direct AF). Total ~5–6h with airport time. Aug 1–2 at sister's in Paris, Aug 3 CDG → KRK as planned.

Rental car (recommended for Stop 2)

The Costa de la Luz beaches aren't reachable by train — buses run but rigid. Pick up a 7-seater at Cádiz station or Sevilla airport on arrival, drop in Ronda. ~€300–500 for 5 days.
