This is the Portugal arc tuned for what you said you want: wild sandy Atlantic beaches, hiking trails, nature, low tourist crowds. We skip the cruise-ship side of the Algarve (Albufeira/Vilamoura) and stay on the two ends — the quiet eastern Ria Formosa estuary and the wind-scoured western Costa Vicentina, with a Lagos middle leg for the iconic cliff coves and family-friendly base.

Why this beats the busy Algarve: Tavira has sandbar beaches you ferry to (locals + retirees, no party crowd), Sagres/Costa Vicentina is a UNESCO-pending nature park with surf villages instead of resorts, and the Ria Formosa is a flamingo-and-salt-pan wonderland that 95% of Algarve visitors never see. Hiking on the Rota Vicentina trail is one of Europe's best coastal walks.

Stop 1 · Tavira & Ria Formosa

Jul 21 – 25 · 4 nights · Eastern Algarve · Moorish riverside town · sandbar beaches · Ria Formosa Natural Park

Sandbar beaches Ria Formosa Natural Park Salt-pan + estuary trails Moorish hilltop castle

Why here

Tavira is the quiet Algarve. Roman bridge, Moorish castle, whitewashed lanes, and a fishing/salt-pan economy that still runs alongside tourism instead of being overrun by it. The beaches are different too: instead of cliff coves, they're barrier-island sandbars that you reach by 5-minute ferry from the town — Ilha de Tavira is 11 km of empty soft sand with pine forest behind it. The Ria Formosa is a UNESCO-grade wetland teeming with flamingos, oystercatchers, and seahorses.

Day by day

Tue Jul 21 · Arrival
  • Morning flight CDG → Faro · 2h45 direct AF/Vueling/TAP
  • Faro airport → Tavira: train via Faro (45 min total, ~€5 pp) — buy CP regional ticket on the platform
  • Check in, walk the Roman bridge, climb to the Moorish castle for sunset
  • Dinner at Tasca Lusa or Aquasul — Algarve seafood: cataplana, sardines, octopus rice
Wed Jul 22 · Ilha de Tavira beach day
  • 5-min ferry from Tavira to Ilha de Tavira — 11 km of soft sand, walk 10 min from the ferry to find your patch of empty beach
  • Lunch at O Pirata on the island — grilled fish on a sandy terrace
  • Afternoon walking the sand spit — dolphins, terns, no buildings
  • Late ferry back, evening tapas at Tavira Lounge
Thu Jul 23 · Ria Formosa nature day
  • Drive or train 25 min to Olhão for the Ria Formosa Natural Park visitor centre at Quinta de Marim
  • Walk the nature trail through salt pans, fresh-water lakes, dune oak forest — flamingos in summer, chameleons if you're lucky (Europe's only wild chameleon population)
  • Lunch in Olhão — fishing-port town, less polished than Tavira, the Saturday fish market is legendary
  • Optional: ferry from Olhão to Culatra or Armona — even quieter sandbar islands
Fri Jul 24 · Cacela Velha + salt-pan hike
  • 15 min drive E to Cacela Velha — tiny cliff-top hamlet overlooking a tidal lagoon, possibly the most beautiful view in the Algarve
  • Walk down to the beach (wade across at low tide — check tide chart) for empty sand and oyster lunch at Casa do Pedro
  • Afternoon back in Tavira — slow swim, gelato at Casa Matias
  • Final dinner at O Tonel — Portuguese grilled meats in a cellar setting
Sat Jul 25 — transit day: Tavira → Lagos · 2h 30m on the Linha do Algarve (CP regional, ~€10 pp, ~€50 family) · direct train along the south coast. Settle in Lagos by lunch.

Stop 2 · Lagos & Ponta da Piedade

Jul 25 – 28 · 3 nights · Western Algarve · cliff coves · Old Town · Ponta da Piedade sea caves

Cliff coves + sandy beaches Lagos Old Town Sea caves + Ponta da Piedade Cliff-top walking trails

Why here

Lagos is the family-friendly middle: a small walled Old Town with a working marina, surrounded by the iconic Algarve cliff coves you've seen in every photo — Praia Dona Ana, Praia do Camilo, Praia do Pinhão — plus the dramatic sandstone formations of Ponta da Piedade. Slightly more developed than Tavira, but still walkable, and the perfect midway base before the wild western coast.

Day by day

Sat Jul 25 · Arrival + Old Town
  • Train Tavira → Lagos (2h 30m), 10 min walk from station to most central Airbnbs
  • Lunch at A Forja — local lunch spot, hearty Portuguese cooking
  • Wander the Old Town walls, Slave Market Museum (a sobering 15-minute pause — Lagos was Europe's first slave market)
  • Sunset at Praia da Batata right under the Old Town
Sun Jul 26 · Cliff coves day
  • Morning walk along the cliff path from Praia da Batata to Praia Dona Ana (20 min, gentle, swim spots along the way)
  • Continue to Praia do Camilo and the stairs down to its sandy cove
  • Lunch at Mar D'Estórias rooftop in the Old Town
  • Late afternoon Ponta da Piedade boat or kayak tour through the sea caves — books at the marina for ~€20 pp
Mon Jul 27 · Sagres preview + Praia da Luz
  • Easy morning at Praia da Luz — 10 min bus W of Lagos, classic family beach with calm water
  • Lunch at Restaurante Mar Bravo in Praia da Luz
  • Optional 30-min bus to Sagres for an afternoon teaser at Cabo de São Vicente (Europe's southwesternmost point) — sunset there is legendary
  • Final Lagos dinner at Adega da Marina — house white, grilled sardines, the works
Tue Jul 28 — transit day: Lagos → Sagres · 50-min bus on Vamus line (~€4 pp, €20 family, direct). No train this leg — Sagres is bus-only. Pack a book.

Stop 3 · Sagres & Costa Vicentina

Jul 28 – Aug 1 · 4 nights · Spain's southwest tip · UNESCO-status nature park · wild Atlantic coast · surfer villages

Wild Atlantic beaches Costa Vicentina Natural Park Rota Vicentina · Fisherman's Trail Cabo de São Vicente lighthouse

Why here

This is the wild Portugal. The Costa Vicentina is a 110-km strip of cliff coastline from Sagres north into the Alentejo — protected as a Natural Park, no resort development allowed, just sandy beaches at the base of cliffs, surfer villages, and the Rota Vicentina walking trail (the "Fisherman's Trail" hugs the coast on dirt paths used by fishermen for 400 years). Sagres itself is a small, sun-weathered surf town at the end of the world.

Day by day

Tue Jul 28 · Arrival + Cabo de São Vicente sunset
  • Bus Lagos → Sagres (50 min), settle in
  • Lunch at A Sagres in the harbour — barnacle (percebes) appetizer if you're brave
  • Drive or bus 6 km to Cabo de São Vicente — Europe's most southwesterly point, 75m cliffs, working lighthouse, sunset over the Atlantic with nothing between you and America
  • Dinner at Mum's back in Sagres — small place, surfer crowd, fresh-fish bowls
Wed Jul 29 · Praia do Beliche + Sagres fort
  • Morning at Praia do Beliche — protected cove with soft sand and Atlantic surf, 5 min from Sagres
  • Lunch in town, then visit the Fortaleza de Sagres — Henry the Navigator's school of navigation, walk the wind-rose compass laid out on the headland
  • Afternoon at Praia do Tonel for surf-watching or beach time
Thu Jul 30 · Rota Vicentina hiking day
  • Drive or taxi 15 km N to Carrapateira / Praia da Bordeira trailhead
  • Walk a section of the Fisherman's Trail — flat, dramatic cliff-top dirt path, 3–4h to Praia do Amado and back · stork nests on rocks, fishermen still casting from cliffs
  • Lunch at Sítio do Rio in Carrapateira — fish stew (caldeirada)
  • Beach afternoon at Praia da Bordeira — a vast tidal sand plain with a river-mouth lagoon (kids love wading the river)
  • Drive back to Sagres for sunset on the cliffs
Fri Jul 31 · Slow day + boat trip
  • Morning boat trip from Sagres marina — dolphin watching + sea-bird colonies along the cliffs (book at the marina, 2h, ~€30 pp)
  • Lunch at Café Correia for a final cataplana
  • Lazy beach afternoon at Praia da Mareta in town
  • Final sunset at Cabo de São Vicente — bring port wine
Sat Aug 1 — exit: Bus Sagres → Lagos (50 min) · train Lagos → Faro (1h 45m) · taxi 15 min to Faro airport · fly FAO → CDG (2h 45m direct AF). Total ~5–6h. Aug 1–2 at sister's in Paris, Aug 3 CDG → KRK as planned.

Trains + bus

Book all rail legs on CP Comboios de Portugal. Bus to Sagres on Vamus Algarve.

  • Faro airport → Tavira (train + airport bus): ~€8 pp · €40 family
  • Tavira → Lagos (CP regional along Algarve line): ~€10 pp · €50 family · 2h 30m direct
  • Lagos → Sagres (Vamus bus, the one bus leg): ~€4 pp · €20 family · 50 min
  • Sagres → Lagos → Faro airport (return): ~€18 pp · €90 family
  • Total ground transport: ~€200 / ~$300 CAD

Optional: rental car for Sagres / Costa Vicentina stop only

Trail heads at Carrapateira and Praia da Bordeira (Rota Vicentina) are technically reachable by infrequent bus or by hitchhiking but a small rental for the 4 days in Sagres opens up the whole Costa Vicentina. Pick up at Lagos station on Jul 28, drop in Lagos on return. ~€150–250 for 4 days.
