This arc hits 5 of the 6 famous Portugal mainland beaches you listed: Ursa (Sintra), Galapinhos (Arrábida), Marinha + Carvalho + Falésia (Algarve). We fly into Lisbon, base near Cascais for the Sintra cluster, train down to Setúbal for the Arrábida Natural Park, then carry on to the Algarve cove zone for the finale. Stop 1 · Cascais & Sintra

Jul 21 – 24 · 3 nights · Coastal Cascais base · Sintra hilltop palaces · Ursa Beach + Praia da Adraga + Cabo da Roca

Ursa · wild cliff beach Praia da Adraga Sintra UNESCO palaces Cabo da Roca · westernmost point

Why here

Cascais is a sun-bleached coastal town 40 min by train W of Lisbon — sandy in-town beaches, marina, real working old quarter. It's the perfect base for the Sintra UNESCO area: fairytale palaces (Pena, Quinta da Regaleira), the dramatic Cabo da Roca cliffs (Europe's westernmost point), and the wild Atlantic beaches that nobody quite manages to crowd — Ursa Beach (you hike down a cliff path to reach it) and Praia da Adraga (similar but easier access).

Day by day

Tue Jul 21 · Arrival + Cascais beach evening
  • Morning flight CDG → Lisbon · 2h45 direct on AF/TAP
  • Train Lisbon airport → Cais do Sodré → Cascais line: 40 min (~€5 pp)
  • Check in, walk the seafront promenade to Praia da Rainha or Praia da Conceição for first swim
  • Dinner at Mar do Inferno overlooking the Boca do Inferno cliff blowhole
Wed Jul 22 · Sintra palace day
  • 30-min train Cascais → Sintra (transfer in Lisbon, or take the direct bus 403)
  • Morning at Pena Palace — early entry to beat heat (book online ahead)
  • Lunch in Sintra Old Town at Tascantiga
  • Afternoon at Quinta da Regaleira — gardens, esoteric symbolism, the spiral initiation well
  • Sunset at Cabo da Roca — Europe's westernmost cliffs, 140m above the Atlantic
Thu Jul 23 · Ursa Beach hike
  • 20-min drive (or bus + walk) N from Cascais to the trailhead above Praia da Ursa
  • The hike down: 30-min steep cliff trail to the beach — not for tiny kids, but doable from age 8+ with care. The reward: a wild crescent of sand cradled by a giant rock arch and stacks, often nearly empty
  • Pack water, snacks, swim gear — no facilities at the beach
  • Hike back up (45 min), drive 10 min to Praia da Adraga for easier-access wild beach + lunch at the cliff-top café Restaurante Mar Adraga
  • Evening back in Cascais for cocktails at Comoba
Fri Jul 24 — transit day: Cascais → Lisbon Cais do Sodré (40 min) → Lisbon Sete Rios → Setúbal (45 min). Total ~2h door-to-door. Or 30-min direct Fertagus train via 25 de Abril bridge from Lisbon's Roma-Areeiro station.

Stop 2 · Setúbal & Arrábida Natural Park

Jul 24 – 27 · 3 nights · Locals' secret · Galapinhos Beach · Mediterranean-clear water in a protected park

Galapinhos · #1 mainland water clarity Portinho da Arrábida Arrábida Natural Park Setúbal fried-cuttlefish

Why here

This stop is the antidote to mass tourism. The Arrábida Natural Park south of Lisbon is a steep cliff range that drops into a sheltered bay with Mediterranean-clear water — totally unlike anywhere else in Portugal. Galapinhos is consistently the highest-rated mainland beach for water quality (3 m visibility), nestled in a pine-and-cliff bay you have to walk 15 min to reach. Portinho da Arrábida is the postcard fishing-village beach next to it. Setúbal is a working port town with the best choco frito (fried cuttlefish) in Portugal.

Day by day

Fri Jul 24 · Arrival + Galapinhos sunset
  • Settle in Setúbal, lunch Casa do Choco for the fried cuttlefish
  • 20-min drive (or bus 39) to the Arrábida park entrance, then 15-min walk down to Praia de Galapinhos
  • Late-afternoon swim in clear blue water with the white cliffs as backdrop
  • Drive back, dinner at O Beco in Setúbal Old Town
Sat Jul 25 · Portinho + Arrábida hike
  • Morning at Portinho da Arrábida — picture-perfect cove beach with a small fortress on the rocks
  • Lunch at Beira-Mar for fresh grilled fish on the beach
  • Afternoon: Arrábida Natural Park drive — the cliff-top road has lookouts every km. Short hikes from each pull-off
  • Evening jazz at Café 5 Estrelas in Setúbal if the calendar lines up
Sun Jul 26 · Comporta day trip (or 2nd Galapinhos)
  • 10-min car ferry across the Sado estuary to Tróia peninsula, drive S to Comporta — long flat sandy beach, rice fields behind, the hippest seaside village in Portugal (still feels low-key compared to Cascais)
  • Lunch at Sal — toes-in-sand, grilled fish, local Comporta white wine
  • Beach afternoon, ferry back to Setúbal
  • Final dinner at Restaurante Xtoria
Mon Jul 27 — transit day: Setúbal → Lisbon Sete Rios → Lagos · 4h total on Alfa Pendular + Algarve regional. Pack snacks. Or stay in Lisbon for one night between if you want a city break.

Stop 3 · Algarve cove finale · Lagos + Carvoeiro region

Jul 27 – Aug 1 · 5 nights · Lagos base · Praia da Marinha, Carvalho, Dona Ana, Camilo, Falésia day trip

Marinha · #1 in Europe Dona Ana · Camilo · Carvalho Falésia · 6km of red cliffs Benagil sea cave

Why here

The Algarve cove zone is the postcard finale: golden-sand pocket beaches at the base of sandstone cliffs, the famous Benagil sea cave (kayak inside under the natural dome of light), and the 6 km red-cliff sweep of Praia da Falésia. We base in Lagos for the western coves and drive 25 min E for the eastern cluster around Carvoeiro.

Day by day

Mon Jul 27 · Arrival + Dona Ana
  • Train Lisbon → Lagos (4h direct Alfa Pendular)
  • Check in, lunch in Lagos Old Town at A Forja
  • Walk to Praia Dona Ana for first cove sunset
Tue Jul 28 · Western cove cluster
  • Cliff path day: Dona Ana → Camilo → Pinhão
  • Kayak/SUP tour through Ponta da Piedade sea caves (~€25 pp)
  • Long late-afternoon beach at Porto de Mos
Wed Jul 29 · Praia da Marinha
  • 30-min drive E to Praia da Marinha — voted #1 beach in Europe (Michelin)
  • Walk the cliff-top Sete Vales Suspensos trail to neighboring secret coves
  • Lunch at the cliff-top café above Marinha
  • Afternoon at Praia do Carvalho — secret cove accessed by a tunnel through the cliff
Thu Jul 30 · Benagil + Algar Seco
  • Drive to Benagil for the famous sea cave kayak tour — the cathedral-domed cave with sand inside
  • Walk the Algar Seco boardwalk (1 km) through eroded sandstone formations
  • Casual Carvoeiro lunch at Bon Bon (Michelin) or beachfront O Lampião
Fri Jul 31 · Falésia red cliffs
  • 45-min drive E to Praia da Falésia — 6 km uninterrupted sand backed by red-striped cliffs
  • Walk the whole stretch, swim, lunch at a cliff-top restaurant in Olhos d'Água
  • Final beach back in Lagos at sunset, farewell dinner at Casinha do Petisco
Sat Aug 1 — exit: Lagos → Faro train 1h45 + airport bus 15 min · fly FAO → CDG 2h45 direct. Aug 1–2 at sister's in Paris, Aug 3 CDG → KRK as planned.

Logistics & bookings

Flights

  • CDG → LIS (Jul 21): ~$170 pp · $850 family · 2h45 direct AF/TAP
  • FAO → CDG (Aug 1): ~$160 pp · $800 family · 2h45 direct AF
  • Total open-jaw flights: ~$1,650 CAD for 5

Trains

Book on CP Comboios. Open bookings ~2 months ahead.

  • Lisbon airport → Cascais (commuter): ~€5 pp · €25 family · 40 min
  • Cascais → Sintra: direct bus 403 ~€5 pp · 30 min · or train via Lisbon
  • Cascais → Setúbal: ~€10 pp · €50 family · 1h45 with one change
  • Setúbal → Lagos (Alfa Pendular + Algarve regional): ~€30 pp · €150 family · 4h
  • Lagos → Faro: ~€10 pp · €50 family · 1h45
  • Total train spend: ~€325 / ~$485 CAD

Rental car (strongly recommended for the Algarve stop)

Pick up in Lagos on Jul 27, drop at Faro airport Aug 1. ~€200–350 for 5 days. Needed to reach Marinha, Benagil, Falésia.
