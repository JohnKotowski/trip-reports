This arc is built around the Dona Ana aesthetic — golden sand in sheltered coves framed by sandstone cliffs and rock stacks. We anchor at Lagos for the western cove cluster (Dona Ana, Camilo, Pinhão, Ponta da Piedade), shift 25 min east to Carvoeiro for the eastern cluster (Praia da Marinha — voted #1 beach in Europe — Benagil sea cave, Carvalho, Algar Seco), then finish inland at Monchique for the eucalyptus mountains, Foia summit, and thermal springs — the green contrast after a week on the cliffs.

Why this beats the rest of the Algarve: Carvoeiro/Lagoa coast is noticeably quieter than Albufeira/Vilamoura (no resorts, smaller towns) while having the most photogenic beaches in Portugal. Monchique is 30 min inland but feels like a different country — cooler air, mountain food, no tourist crowd.

Stop 1 · Lagos & the western cove cluster

Jul 21 – 25 · 4 nights · Walled Old Town, marina, six cove beaches walkable or short drive from base

Praia Dona Ana Camilo · Pinhão · Porto de Mos Ponta da Piedade sea caves Walled Old Town

Why here

Lagos is the family-friendly Algarve cove hub. Praia Dona Ana sits 10 min walk from the Old Town and is the cover-photo Algarve beach you've seen everywhere. Within 10 min by foot or short taxi you also have Camilo, Pinhão, Porto de Mos, and the dramatic Ponta da Piedade headland with its kayak-accessible sea caves.

Day by day

Tue Jul 21 · Arrival
  • Morning flight CDG → Faro · 2h45 direct AF/TAP/Vueling
  • Train Faro → Lagos · 1h45 along the Algarve line (~€10 pp)
  • Check in, lunch at A Forja — local lunch spot, generous Portuguese cooking
  • Easy walk to Praia da Batata right under the Old Town walls for first swim
  • Sunset over the marina, dinner at Adega da Marina
Wed Jul 22 · Dona Ana + Camilo day
  • 10-min walk (or 5-min taxi) S to Praia Dona Ana — golden cove flanked by rock stacks, soft sand, calm protected water
  • Cliff path 5 min S to Praia do Camilo — stairs down to a smaller twin cove (200 steps; worth it)
  • Lunch at O Camilo restaurant overlooking the beach
  • Afternoon swim, then the cliff path 10 min further to Praia do Pinhão — the locals' quieter option
Thu Jul 23 · Ponta da Piedade + kayak
  • Walk or short taxi to Ponta da Piedade headland — sandstone cliffs, rock arches, lighthouse
  • Kayak or SUP tour through the sea caves from the marina (~€25 pp, 2h, includes drop-off snorkel stops) — book at the marina day of
  • Lunch back in town at Mar D'Estórias rooftop
  • Long beach afternoon at Porto de Mos — wide sandy + cliff backdrop, 5 min taxi S
Fri Jul 24 · Lazy + Praia da Luz
  • Sleep in. Lazy morning, café in the Old Town square
  • 10-min bus W to Praia da Luz — classic wide family beach with calm water and a sea-side promenade
  • Lunch at Restaurante Mar Bravo
  • Optional afternoon Slave Market Museum (the 15-min sobering history lesson Lagos owes you)
  • Final Lagos dinner at Casinha do Petisco — book ahead, tiny, hand-made tapas
Sat Jul 25 — transit day: Lagos → Carvoeiro · 25-min train Lagos→Estômbar/Lagoa station + 15-min taxi or bus (~€15) to Carvoeiro. Total ~1h door-to-door. Easy.

Stop 2 · Carvoeiro & the eastern cove cluster

Jul 25 – 29 · 4 nights · Quieter than Lagos · Praia da Marinha + Benagil + Carvalho + Algar Seco

Praia da Marinha (#1 in Europe) Praia do Carvalho · hidden cove Benagil sea cave Algar Seco coastal walk

Why here

Carvoeiro is a former fishing village that became a small low-key beach town. The coastline E of here has the most concentrated set of dramatic cove beaches in Portugal — Praia da Marinha (consistently voted top beach in Europe), Praia do Carvalho (hidden, accessed through a tunnel cut in the cliff), the Benagil sea cave (kayak through the cave's beach inside the cliff), and the Algar Seco rock formations you can walk through on a boardwalk.

Day by day

Sat Jul 25 · Arrival + Algar Seco walk
  • Settle in Carvoeiro, lunch at Bon Bon Michelin spot (book ahead) or casual O Lampião
  • Walk the Algar Seco boardwalk — 1 km coastal path through eroded sandstone formations, swim spots along the way
  • Evening swim at Praia do Carvoeiro right in town
  • Dinner at Restaurante O Castelo with cliff-edge sunset
Sun Jul 26 · Praia da Marinha
  • 15-min drive E to Praia da Marinha — park at the top, descend the wooden stairs to soft sand and clear turquoise water
  • The Sete Vales Suspensos (Seven Hanging Valleys) trail starts here — easy cliff-top walk to other secret beaches if you want a hike day
  • Lunch at the cliff-top café above Marinha
  • Afternoon back in Carvoeiro, swim at Praia do Paraíso (the local hidden cove)
Mon Jul 27 · Benagil sea cave
  • 10-min drive E to Benagil village
  • Kayak or SUP tour into the Benagil sea cave — the cathedral-domed cave with its own beach inside, sunlight pouring through the natural ceiling oculus · book a guided tour from Benagil beach (~€30 pp, 1.5h)
  • Lunch at O Pescador on Benagil beach
  • Afternoon at Praia do Carvalho — 5 min E, access via tunnel cut in the cliff, secluded soft-sand cove
Tue Jul 28 · Falésia day trip
  • Drive 30 min E to Praia da Falésia — 6 km uninterrupted sand backed by dramatic red-and-white cliff striped formations · walk the whole stretch if you've got it in you
  • Lunch at one of the cliff-top restaurants in Olhos d'Água village
  • Back to Carvoeiro late afternoon for a final swim
  • Farewell dinner at Restaurante Boneca Bar tucked under the Algar Seco cliffs
Wed Jul 29 — transit day: Carvoeiro → Monchique · 15-min taxi to Lagoa or Portimão station, then 45-min bus on Vamus from Portimão → Monchique town (~€5 pp). Total ~1h30. Or rent a car for this leg — much easier in the mountains.

Stop 3 · Monchique mountains

Jul 29 – Aug 1 · 3 nights · Interior Algarve · cooler air · eucalyptus and cork-oak forest · Foia summit · thermal springs

Foia summit (902m) Serra de Monchique forest Caldas de Monchique thermal springs Mountain villages + medronho

Why here

After a week of cove beaches, Monchique is the green palate cleanser. The Serra de Monchique is the only real mountain range in the Algarve — eucalyptus, cork-oak, and chestnut forest, with Foia as the highest peak (902m, panoramic view from the Algarve coast to the Alentejo plains). Caldas de Monchique is a tiny thermal-spring spa village in a hidden valley with Roman-era hot baths. The pace shifts: cooler air, slower meals, local medronho (strawberry-tree firewater).

Day by day

Wed Jul 29 · Arrival + Caldas de Monchique
  • Arrive Monchique, settle in (Airbnbs in old farmhouses on the hillsides are the move)
  • 5 km S to Caldas de Monchique — Roman-era thermal spring village in a steep wooded valley
  • Hot springs and spa at Termas de Monchique (book ahead in summer) — soak, then dinner in the tiny plaza below
Thu Jul 30 · Foia summit hike
  • Drive 8 km up to Foia, the Algarve's highest point (902m) — on clear mornings you see Cabo de São Vicente, the coast, and N to the Alentejo plains
  • Hike: the loop from Foia down to the springs of the Rio Boina is ~3h, dropping through laurel forest
  • Easy alternative: drive to Foia, walk the short summit loop (30 min), then a relaxed lunch at Restaurante a Charrette in Monchique village
  • Afternoon market in Monchique — local cheese, honey, medronho, smoked meats
Fri Jul 31 · Slow day
  • Easy hike from Monchique town to the convent ruins (Nossa Senhora do Desterro) — 30-min uphill, eucalyptus shade, views E into the Algarve
  • Lunch at Restaurante O Luar da Foia — mountain piri-piri chicken, local red wine
  • Optional: one more thermal soak at Caldas, or drive down to Praia da Rocha (45 min) for a final coast beach day
  • Final mountain dinner at Jardim das Oliveiras
Sat Aug 1 — exit: Bus Monchique → Portimão (45 min) · train Portimão → Faro (1h) · taxi 15 min to Faro airport · fly FAO → CDG (2h45 direct AF). Total ~5h. Aug 1–2 at sister's, Aug 3 CDG → KRK as planned.

Optional: rental car

For Stop 2 (Carvoeiro) and Stop 3 (Monchique), a car opens up Marinha/Benagil/Falésia and the Foia drive. Pick up at Lagos on Jul 25, drop at Faro airport on Aug 1. ~€250–400 for 7 days.
