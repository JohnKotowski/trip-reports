Wed Jul 22
Praia do Barril + the anchor cemetery
Sandbar beachUnique spotShort walk
Morning · nature + the unique thing
  • Drive or train 15 min W to Pedras d'el Rei (Santa Luzia side of Tavira)
  • Walk across the lagoon causeway (10 min) — OR take the little tourist mini-train through the salt-pans (kids love it, ~€2 pp RT)
  • Arrive at Praia do Barril — and the Cemitério das Âncoras ("anchor cemetery"): hundreds of rusted iron tuna-fishing anchors laid out in rows on the dunes behind the beach. A genuinely unforgettable, photogenic, slightly haunting installation that nobody outside the Algarve knows about. Unique spot
  • Beach morning — soft sand, calm Atlantic, walk W for a quieter stretch
Lunch · toes-in-sand
  • Restaurante Barril right behind the beach — grilled fish, gazpacho jug, cold Vinho Verde
Afternoon · slow + Santa Luzia
  • Walk or train back to Santa Luzia — the octopus capital of Portugal (every restaurant claims so)
  • Stroll the seafront, watch the octopus-pot fishermen unload
  • Optional second swim back on Ilha de Tavira
Dinner
  • Restaurante O Capelo in Santa Luzia — octopus four ways. Or back in Tavira at O Tonel for cellar-setting grills
Thu Jul 23
Ria Formosa nature day + Cacela Velha sunset
Nature parkBoardwalk hikeUnique view
Morning · Ria Formosa Natural Park
  • Drive 25 min W to Quinta de Marim (the park's main visitor centre, near Olhão)
  • Walk the nature trail (3 km loop, ~1h30) — boardwalks through salt-pans, fresh-water lakes, dune-oak forest. Bring binoculars: flamingos, oystercatchers, hoopoes, and (if you're lucky) Europe's only wild chameleons
  • Stop at the historic tide mill on the trail (Moinho da Maré) — restored Phoenician/Roman tech
Lunch · Olhão
  • Olhão's fish market (Sábado / Tuesday) — buy bread, cheese, peaches, and a slice of grilled tuna belly straight from the stalls. Or sit-down at O Bote for cataplana
Afternoon · Cacela Velha + tidal lagoon swim
  • Drive 30 min E to Cacela Velha — a tiny clifftop hamlet of 4 streets and a church, overlooking what might be the most beautiful tidal lagoon view in Portugal
  • Check the tide tables: at low tide, wade across to the empty sand spit on the far side. Bring snorkel masks if you have them — clear shallow water, juvenile fish everywhere
  • Casa do Pedro on the cliff for fresh-shucked oysters with cold Algarvio white
Dinner · fine dining anchor
  • El Campero in nearby Barbate (Spanish side — easy 1h drive, but worth it) for tuna ronqueo — the ritualistic carving of bluefin tuna into 24 different cuts. World-renowned. Book 1+ week ahead. Fine dining
    OR more local: A Ver Tavira back in town for traditional Algarve fine dining

  • Ponta da Piedade kayak or SUP tour — 2h paddle through the sandstone sea caves and arches, with snorkel stops and a guide pointing out hidden coves you can only access from the water (~€25 pp, book day-of or 1 day ahead from any marina shack) Unique

  • Finish at Camilo (200 steps down to a smaller twin cove) for sunset
Dinner

Sun Jul 26
Praia da Marinha + 7 Hanging Valleys coastal hike
#1 beach in EuropeCoastal hikeBenagil cave
Morning · the iconic coastal hike
  • 30-min drive E to Praia da Marinha (voted #1 beach in Europe by Michelin)
  • Park at the top, before the heat starts the Sete Vales Suspensos (Seven Hanging Valleys) trail — 12 km point-to-point along the clifftop from Marinha to Praia da Vale Centianes, but you can walk just the first 3 km section to Benagil and turn around (~2h round trip). Easy underfoot, dramatic cliff views the whole way, swimmable secret beaches at the base of each "hanging valley"
Late morning · the cave
  • From Benagil, hop on a kayak or SUP tour into the Benagil sea cave — the famous cathedral-domed cave with sand inside and sunlight pouring through the natural oculus (~€30 pp, 1h, book at the beach) Unique
Lunch · toes in sand
  • O Pescador on Benagil beach — grilled fresh fish, gazpacho, on a sandy terrace
Afternoon · hidden cove
  • 10-min drive to Praia do Carvalho — a secluded golden-sand cove reached through a tunnel cut in the cliff. Less crowded than Marinha
  • Late swim, drive back to Lagos before sunset
Dinner · optional fine dining
  • Bon Bon (Carvoeiro, Michelin star) — chef Louis Anjos, very modern Portuguese, ~€140 pp tasting menu. Book 2+ weeks ahead. Fine dining
    OR simpler back in Lagos: Vivendi for the wine list
Mon Jul 27
Sagres + Cabo de São Vicente · Europe's end
Wild AtlanticCosta VicentinaWesternmost point
Morning · drive west to land's end
  • 30-min drive W to Sagres — wind-scoured surf village at the SW corner of mainland Europe
  • Stop at Praia do Beliche first — sheltered cove with soft sand, Atlantic swell, climb down 80 wooden stairs (worth it). Or Praia do Tonel for surfer-watching
Late morning · clifftop walk + Cabo
  • Drive 6 km W to Cabo de São Vicente — Europe's most southwesterly point, 75m cliffs, working lighthouse
  • Walk a short section of the Rota Vicentina / Fisherman's Trail along the cliffs — flat, dramatic, fishermen still casting from terrifying cliff-edge positions
  • Buy "the last sausage roll before America" from the tiny food truck at the lighthouse (it's an actual thing, they lean into it)
Lunch · the famous percebes
  • A Tasca in Sagres harbour — order percebes (gooseneck barnacles, weird-looking, transformative). Add a tuna steak the size of a hardback book
Afternoon · Carrapateira wild beach + sunset
  • 20-min drive N to Praia da Bordeira / Carrapateira — a vast tidal sand plain with a river-mouth lagoon (kids love wading the river). Wild Atlantic surf, almost empty, dunes
  • Drive back to Cabo de São Vicente for sunset — the absolute end of Europe. Bring a thermos of wine
Dinner · back in Lagos
  • Casinha do Petisco if you didn't go on Friday — tiny menu, hand-cooked, very good

Stop 3 · Cádiz & El Puerto de Santa María

Jul 28–Aug 1 · 4 nights · 3000-year-old port city · Bolonia dune climb · Doñana wildlife · Sierra de Grazalema hiking

Wed Jul 29
Bolonia dune climb + Baelo Claudia ruins on the beach
Wild dunesDune climbRoman city on the sand
Morning · drive S
  • 1h drive S to Bolonia — one of the wildest, widest sandy beaches in Spain
  • Climb the giant Bolonia dune — 30m tall sand mountain at the south end of the beach. Photogenic and absurd. Kids love it.
  • Long swim, the Atlantic is warm here in late July
Late morning · the unique thing
  • Baelo Claudia Roman ruins right behind Bolonia beach — an entire 1st-century fish-salting town preserved on the sand. Wander the forum, the theatre, the salting tanks. Free for EU citizens, ~€2 otherwise Unique spot
Lunch · toes in sand
  • Las Rejas at the south end of Bolonia beach — open-air, grilled fish, gazpacho jugs, terrible service in the best way
Afternoon · Zahara de los Atunes
  • 15-min drive E to Zahara de los Atunes — fishing village + 8 km of soft sand. Walk far from the village for empty beach. The Atlantic dunes here are the same coast that runs west into the Algarve
  • Drive back to Cádiz/El Puerto for sunset
Dinner · the headline meal
  • Aponiente in El Puerto de Santa María — chef Ángel León, "Chef del Mar". 3 Michelin stars. Tasting menu built entirely around the sea — sea-grown vegetables, plankton, brand-new species he's pulled from local waters. One of the world's most singular restaurants. ~€280 pp, book 6–8 weeks ahead minimum. Fine dining
Thu Jul 30
Sierra de Grazalema · hiking day inland
Mountain hikeNatural parkWhite village
Morning · drive into the sierra
  • 1h30 drive NE to the Sierra de Grazalema Natural Park — a limestone range full of cork-oak forest, vulture vistas, and Mediterranean-mountain hiking
  • Park visitor centre at El Bosque · pick up trail maps
  • Trail option (family): Río Majaceite from Benamahoma → El Bosque · 4.5 km along a shaded river, mostly flat, swimmable river-pool stops
  • Trail option (moderate): Salto del Cabrero loop — 12 km, limestone cliffs, vulture sightings, half-day
Lunch · in the white village
  • Cádiz El Chico in Grazalema village — try the local borreguito en salsa (lamb), or trout from the mountain rivers
Afternoon · Setenil de las Bodegas
  • 30-min drive to Setenil de las Bodegas — pueblo blanco built UNDER massive rock overhangs. Houses, restaurants, streets literally tucked beneath cliffs Unique
  • Walk the rock-cellar streets, ice cream + photos
Dinner · back in Cádiz
  • Casa Manteca in La Viña — the iconic chicharrones de Cádiz, manzanilla sherry from Sanlúcar, flamenco on the walls
Fri Jul 31
Doñana boat trip + Sanlúcar sherry sunset
Doñana wildlifeWild horses + flamingosSherry country
Morning · the wildest place in Spain
  • 30-min drive N to Sanlúcar de Barrameda · catch the Buque Real Fernando boat trip across the Guadalquivir to Doñana National Park · 3h cruise + guided walks ashore (~€20 pp adult, book 2 days ahead at fundaciondonana.es)
  • Doñana is Spain's most important wetland — pink flamingos, wild horses, Iberian lynx (rarely seen), the largest stork colony in Europe. UNESCO-protected. You can't drive in
Lunch · the famous one
  • Casa Bigote in Sanlúcar — beachfront, langostinos de Sanlúcar (the world's best prawns, no exaggeration — they have their own appellation), manzanilla sherry from across the street. Book ahead, popular.
Afternoon · sherry bodega tour
  • Visit one of the manzanilla bodegas in Sanlúcar — Bodega Barbadillo (the biggest, more touristy) or Bodega Hidalgo La Gitana (smaller, family). Tour + tasting ~€15 pp
  • Walk along the seafront promenade past the Bajo de Guía fishermen's restaurants
Evening · sunset over Doñana
  • Sunset drinks at Bajo de Guía promenade — the only place in Spain where the sun sets over a national park across the river. Chiringuito (beach bar) with cold manzanilla in a fino glass Iconic
  • Drive back to Cádiz/El Puerto
Dinner · the final feast
  • If you went to Aponiente already: Toro Tapas in Cádiz (Mauricio Giraldo's place, Michelin Bib Gourmand) — bull's-tail, Iberian pork
  • OR a final stop at Restaurante La Curiosa in El Puerto — small, sherry-paired tapas
