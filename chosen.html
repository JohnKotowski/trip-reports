Stop 1 · Tavira & Cabanas

Jul 21 – 24 · 3 nights · Eastern Algarve · sandbar beaches via ferry · Ria Formosa lagoon nature

Ilha de Tavira sandbar Ria Formosa Natural Park Moorish hilltop castle Cataplana + Algarve grilled fish

Why here

Quiet, working Algarve town. Roman bridge, Moorish castle, whitewashed lanes. Beach access via 5-minute ferry to Ilha de Tavira — 11 km of empty soft sand. The Ria Formosa Natural Park stretches W from here with salt pans, flamingos, and seahorses. Cabanas (5 km E) is the alternate base — quieter again, dedicated ferry to its own sandbar.

Stop 2 · Lagos · Burgau · Salema

Jul 24 – 28 · 4 nights · Western Algarve · postcard cove beaches · Ponta da Piedade · Burgau/Salema fishing villages

Praia Dona Ana · Camilo · Pinhão Praia da Luz · Burgau · Salema Ponta da Piedade sea caves Walled Old Town + Slave Market

Why here

Lagos is the cove-beach hub: Praia Dona Ana (postcard golden cove), Camilo (down 200 stairs), Pinhão (locals' quieter pick), and the cliff-walking trail along Ponta da Piedade with its sandstone arches. Burgau and Salema are smaller fishing villages 15–20 min W with their own sandy bays — quieter than Lagos town, perfect if you want a beach-village base instead of a tourist hub.

Stop 3 · Cádiz & El Puerto de Santa María

Jul 28 – Aug 1 · 4 nights · Costa de la Luz · 3000-year-old port city + wider Atlantic sandy beaches

La Caleta · Cádiz city beach Bolonia dunes · Zahara (day trip) Cádiz Old Town · Cathedral Sherry from Jerez · tuna ronqueo

Why here

Cádiz is the oldest continuously inhabited city in Western Europe — Phoenician port still humming. La Caleta is the in-city beach (the Halle Berry James Bond beach). El Puerto de Santa María (across the bay, 30 min ferry or 15-min AVE train) is the sherry-bodega town and a quieter base. Day trips: Bolonia (1h drive, wide sandy beach with the giant dune you climb), Jerez (bodega tours), Sanlúcar de Barrameda (manzanilla sherry + horse races on the beach).
