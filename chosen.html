Four nights of Algarve cove beaches in Lagos. Two nights in Sevilla — the cultural anchor: Cathedral, Real Alcázar, flamenco — done in the cool morning hours, slow afternoons. Five nights in Granada, base for the Alhambra (one of the world's most extraordinary buildings) and Sierra Nevada hiking — mainland Spain's highest range, with day-trips into the Las Alpujarras white villages on the south face.

Why this works for nature/hiking priority: Sierra Nevada has serious hiking — Mulhacén (3,479m, mainland Spain's highest peak) is a one-day refuge-supported climb; the south-face Alpujarras villages (Capileira, Bubión, Pampaneira) sit at 1,400m with networked trails through chestnut forest and Berber-style stone-irrigation channels. Way more dramatic than the Algarve interior, and August is when you actually want to be in the cool mountain air.

Stop 3 · Granada & Sierra Nevada

Jul 27–Aug 1 · 5 nights · Alhambra + Albaicín + Sierra Nevada high-altitude hiking + Las Alpujarras white villages

Tue Jul 28
Alhambra · the building itself
UNESCO masterpieceNasrid Palaces
Morning · Alhambra timed entry
  • Book months ahead at alhambra-patronato.es — Nasrid Palaces have a strict timed entry slot, sells out 6–8 weeks ahead in summer. Get the 8:30 or 9am slot to beat heat + crowds
  • Walking sequence: Alcazaba fortress (military), Nasrid Palaces (the masterpiece — Court of the Lions, Hall of the Ambassadors), Generalife gardens. Plan 4h on-site
Lunch + siesta
  • Drop down to El Trillo in Albaicín — small terrace, Granadino cuisine. Long lunch + nap
Evening · Sacromonte + zambra flamenco
  • Walk to Sacromonte neighborhood — the historic Roma/gitano caves dug into the hillside
  • Cave flamenco at Cuevas Los Tarantos or Venta El Gallo — this is the original zambra style, raw, performed inside whitewashed cave rooms Unique
Wed Jul 29
Sierra Nevada hike day · Hoya de la Mora / Veleta
3,000m+ alpine hikeNational park
Morning · drive up
  • 45-min drive (or shuttle bus) from Granada up to Hoya de la Mora at 2,500m — the high trailhead
  • Hike to Pico Veleta (3,396m, mainland Spain's 4th-highest) — 4h round trip, ~700m vertical, technically class-1 walking. Or do the easier 2h loop around Laguna de las Yeguas
  • If you want the headline summit: Mulhacén (3,479m) is a longer, more committed day — typically done from the Refugio Poqueira (1 night refuge stay) or via the high-altitude shuttle from Capileira. Doable as a one-day push but starts pre-dawn
Lunch · mountain refuge
  • Eat from the pack at the summit, or descend to Albergue Universitario at Hoya de la Mora for hot soup
Afternoon · descent + thermal bath
  • Back in Granada by 4pm — book a session at Hammam Al Ándalus, the modern reproduction of the medieval Arab baths (~€40 pp, 90 min, advance booking required)
Dinner
  • Carmela Restaurante — modern Andalusian tapas, near the Cathedral
Thu Jul 30
Las Alpujarras · Berber-irrigation white villages
Village-to-village trailBerber heritageMountain cuisine
Morning · drive S over the sierra
  • 1h30 drive S from Granada into Las Alpujarras — the south face of Sierra Nevada, with Berber-style stone irrigation channels (acequias) that haven't changed in 800 years
  • Stop at Pampaneira (1,058m) for chocolate at Abuela Ili, then continue to Bubión + Capileira (1,436m) — the highest three of the Poqueira gorge villages
Late morning · village-to-village hike
  • Walk the classic Poqueira gorge loop: Capileira → down to the river → up to Bubión → back to Capileira (~3h, 9 km, ~400m descent + climb). Chestnut forest, irrigation acequias, Sierra Nevada views the whole way
  • Alternative shorter: Pampaneira → Bubión via the GR-7 trail (1h30, mostly downhill)
Lunch · the mountain plate
  • La Fragua II in Trevélez (highest village in Spain, 1,476m) — order the jamón de Trevélez (mountain-cured ham, the cold dry air does the curing), local trout, ajoblanco, Alpujarran wine. Worth the extra 20-min drive E
Afternoon · slow descent
  • Drive back N stopping at the Mirador del Cerecillo lookout
  • Optional: Lanjarón (water-bottling town, mineral hot springs)
Dinner · back in Granada
  • Damasqueros — modern Granadino tasting menu, small, well-priced, the chef's mother is from Sevilla and the abuela is from Granada · or simpler: rotate tapas bars in Albaicín for free-tapa night #2
